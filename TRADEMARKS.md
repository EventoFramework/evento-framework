# Evento Framework — Trademark Policy

The code of Evento Framework is public ([LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt)); its names are not. This policy
covers the names **"Evento"**, **"Evento Framework"** and **"RECQ"** (the architecture Evento
implements), and the Evento logos (together, "the Marks"), which belong to Gabor Galazzo.

Licences cover code, not names, and not ideas: anyone may study the RECQ architecture and apply it,
with or without Evento. What this policy protects is the use of the Marks to sell something.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your application or product is built with, runs on, or is compatible with Evento, in
  plain words ("built with Evento Framework").
- Write, teach and talk about Evento and RECQ: articles, books, university courses, conference talks,
  videos, free workshops, open-source examples.
- Name Evento and RECQ on your CV or in a job advert, and describe your experience with them.
- Refer to the project in a fork or a modified version you share for free, as long as its name is
  not "Evento" (say "based on Evento Framework").

## You may not, without written authorisation

- Use the Marks in the name, domain, logo or marketing of a commercial product or service: hosting
  or managed Evento, consulting, support, training, certification, or architecture services sold as
  being about Evento or RECQ ("Evento consulting", "RECQ architecture audits", "Certified RECQ
  architect").
- Distribute a modified version of the software under the name "Evento".
- Suggest that the Licensor endorses, certifies, supports or is a partner of you or your offering.
- Register a company, product, domain or social media name containing the Marks.

Authorisations are given case by case, through the agreement in
[LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.txt](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.txt). Ask at
[eventoframework.com/licensing](https://www.eventoframework.com/licensing) or
[gabor.galazzo@gmail.com](mailto:gabor.galazzo@gmail.com).
